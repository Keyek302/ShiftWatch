Alerts & widgets

Keep your “to go” amount on the home-screen app icon while you work.

Goal on app icon

Shows whole dollars left to a 10-hour shift.

Live notification

Keeps “$ to go” and time left in your notification shade while a shift runs.

Notify at goal

A one-time alert when you hit the full shift.

Shows money to go and time to go in a small window that floats over other apps. Tapping the TO GO box at the top does the same. App icons can only show a number, so the icon badge carries dollars left. The live notification carries dollars and time left. A website can’t draw a true home-screen widget. The app icon badge needs Shift Watch installed to your home screen. Notifications need permission and only arrive while the app is open or in the background.

Backup & restore

Your shifts live only in this browser. Save a backup so you can move to a new phone or recover after clearing data.

If your browser blocks downloads, use Copy backup text and paste it into a note. Importing adds shifts that aren’t already in the app and restores the settings in the backup.
